# CertKeep: Your Compliance Hub

Build a polished, responsive SaaS landing page for a construction compliance product with the working name “CertKeep.”

IMPORTANT SCOPE:
Build only the marketing landing page and pilot signup experience. Do not build the full dashboard, customer authentication, payment processing, document storage, or subcontractor portal yet.

PRODUCT:
CertKeep helps small commercial general contractors collect, review, and track subcontractor insurance certificates, licenses, W-9s, and other compliance documents.

CORE DIFFERENTIATOR:
The contractor sends one secure link. The subcontractor opens it on their phone, uploads a photo or PDF, enters the expiration date, and submits it without creating an account or password.

TARGET CUSTOMER:
Small and midsize commercial general contractors with approximately 10–100 active subcontractors who currently use spreadsheets, emails, and shared folders.

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Create an original visual identity inspired by the refinement, spacing, product presentation, and premium SaaS quality of Vanta, combined with the construction-specific clarity of Billy. Do not copy either company’s layouts, wording, illustrations, branding, colors, or assets.

The finished design should feel like a custom Webflow site designed by a high-end SaaS agency—not a generic AI template.

FAQ SECTION:
Include these questions:

Do subcontractors need an account?
Answer: No. They receive a secure upload link that works from a phone or computer.

Does CertKeep verify insurance coverage?
Answer: The initial version helps collect, organize, and review documents. Contractors remain responsible for determining whether coverage and requirements are acceptable.

Can I import my existing spreadsheet?
Answer: CSV importing is planned so contractors can begin with their existing subcontractor list.

Will there be annual contracts?
Answer: The planned Starter plan is month to month.

Is this a replacement for my insurance broker or attorney?
Answer: No. CertKeep is a document workflow and tracking tool, not an insurance, legal, or regulatory adviser.

When will CertKeep be available?
Answer: A limited pilot is being organized now.
